Encouragement of Climb Locations: Hiking the Real Hanno from Tokyo

If you have watched Encouragement of Climb (Yama no Susume), you may already know that the hills in the story are not invented. The series is set in and around Hanno, a quiet town in Saitama that sits about an hour from central Tokyo. Most visitors who come to Japan for anime stay in Akihabara or Ikebukuro, and very few ever make the trip out to the actual mountains. That is exactly why it works so well as a day out.

Why Hanno suits a pilgrimage

Anime pilgrimages are often a matter of standing in a spot and recognising a frame. Hanno gives you more than that. The town itself, the temple and the summits are all part of one continuous walk, so you are not hopping between disconnected photo spots. You start in the town, climb into the hills, and finish with views over the area the story takes place in.

What the route includes

The walk begins on Hanno Ginza, the shopping street in town. From there we continue to Kannonji Temple, and then climb to the summits of Mt. Tenran and Mt. Tonosu. A local guide goes with you the whole way, so you are not left to work out the trail or the story on your own.

Do you need to be a hiker?

No. It is a short mountain outing rather than an expedition, and it suits fans who want to see the real places and people who simply like a walk. Wear comfortable walking shoes and clothing that suits the weather, and bring water. If you are not sure whether the pace is right for you, ask before booking and we can talk it through.

Getting the most out of the day

Because the group is capped at three or four guests, there is room to stop, take photos, and ask about the places you recognise. If you would like to make a longer day of Hanno, the town also has a Zen temple garden, a fermentation theme park and a sake brewery, which together make up our Hanno sake and fermentation tour. The two tours are separate, so you can choose the one that fits your day, and you can read both tour pages for the details: Hanno Anime Pilgrimage Hike and Hanno Sake & Fermentation Tour.
