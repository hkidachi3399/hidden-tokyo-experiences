Hanno Anime Pilgrimage Hike: Walk the Real Setting of "Encouragement of Climb"

Hanno, a town in Saitama about an hour from Tokyo, is the setting of the anime and manga "Encouragement of Climb" (Yama no Susume). This small-group hike takes you to the places behind the story: the Hanno Ginza shopping street, Kannonji Temple, and the summits of Mt. Tenran and Mt. Tonosu.

It is a 3-hour day trip from Tokyo that very few anime tours ever reach, led by a local guide, with a maximum of four guests.

What the route covers

The walk starts in the town itself on Hanno Ginza, continues to Kannonji Temple, and then climbs to two summits, Mt. Tenran and Mt. Tonosu. You see the town and the hills the story is set in, with a guide who can point out what is what along the way.

Who it is for

Fans of the series who want to see the real places, and walkers who would like a short, easy-to-reach mountain outing from Tokyo. Wear comfortable walking shoes and clothing suited to the weather.
