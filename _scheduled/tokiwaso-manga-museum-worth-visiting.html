Is the Tokiwaso Manga Museum Worth Visiting? A Practical Guide

If you like manga and have already done Akihabara, this is the place to see where it all started.

Tokiwaso was a small two-story apartment building in Shiinamachi, in Toshima City. Osamu Tezuka and the creators of Doraemon lived there as young artists. The museum is a faithful reconstruction of that building, and you can look into recreated rooms of the era.

The basics

  • Open 10:00 to 18:00 (last entry 17:30), closed on Mondays and for exhibit changes. Check the official site before you go.
  • Entry is free, but special exhibitions can require a ticket.
  • It is a 10 to 15 minute walk from Shiinamachi Station on the Seibu Ikebukuro Line.

What to do around it

The streets nearby keep a Showa-era feel. There is a free rest area on Tokiwaso Street and a small manga reading corner, so the walk is part of the visit.

Who it is for

Fans who want the story behind the characters more than merchandise. If you want shopping and game centers, Akihabara fits better.

With a guide

My small-group walk starts at Shiinamachi Station and includes the museum with entry, the streets between the sites, and an optional ramen lunch (paid on site). Up to 4 guests. An extended option adds the Toei Animation Museum in Oizumi-gakuen.
